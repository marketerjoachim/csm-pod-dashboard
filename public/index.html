Glossary

LTV UP

Time = ROAS: CSM believes investing dedicated time in this client will produce strong ad return (ROAS), indicating high lifetime value potential. Prioritize.

FE

Front-End: Needs client-facing CSM involvement (relationship management, meetings, calls).

C-SUITE

C-Suite Escalation: Requires senior leadership involvement to resolve (strategic meeting or escalation).

CHURN

Churn Risk: Workspace flagged with cancelled/overdue status or explicit churn risk indicators.

IDLE

Inactive: No active ad campaigns for 20+ days. Risk of disengagement if not addressed.

INT.ERR

Integration Error: Technical integration with client's store is broken, blocking all value delivery.

UPSELL

Upsell Candidate: Client's usage or business size suggests they should be on a higher plan.

REVSHARE

Revenue Share: Company earns a % of client's profit/revenue, creating aligned growth incentives.

Methodology

Priority Index = 3-Year LTV / CSM Effort, where LTV = ARR x Retention Probability x Growth Multiplier x 3 years.

Retention Probability is derived from health rating (1-5 stars maps to 25%-92%), adjusted down for churn flags, prolonged campaign inactivity, or explicit churn risk comments.

Growth Multiplier (0.5x-2.5x) factors in current and lifetime ROAS performance, Shopify sales volume (proxy for client business size), CSM-flagged "Time=ROAS" signals, and upsell indicators.

CSM Effort (0.5x-2.0x) estimates relative time investment based on integration issues, C-suite escalation needs, client complexity notes, and new client onboarding overhead.

Note: ARR is estimated from plan-level averages, not individual subscription data. 22 of 47 workspaces are unrated and default to 65% retention. Shopify sales are a 30-day snapshot. Model is directionally correct but exact figures will shift with real per-workspace ARR and health ratings.
